## 3. Colors

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | `#00B0A8` | Teal — brand core, secondary buttons |
| Secondary | `#27CFC3` | Lighter teal — highlights, hovers |
| Accent | `#F5A46C` | Warm apricot — primary CTA, links |
| Background (dark) | `#1F1F1F` | Dark sections |
| Text primary | `#000000` | Body copy on light |
| Button text | `#FFFFFF` | On both button colors |

Palette logic: cool teal (trust, accuracy, "goed met cijfers") balanced by a warm human apricot (care, "goed voor mensen"). Never use corporate navy/grey — it would kill the human half of the promise.

## 5. UI system

- **Base spacing unit:** 4px
- **Global border radius:** 5px
- **Primary button:** `#F5A46C` bg, `#FFFFFF` text, fully rounded (200px pill), no shadow — e.g. "Neem contact op"
- **Secondary button:** `#00B0A8` bg, `#FFFFFF` text, pill — e.g. "Dit is Namber"
- **Inputs:** transparent background, square (0px radius), no shadow
- **Shadows:** none anywhere — flat design
- **Framework:** custom (no component library)

## 6. Voice & tone

**Personality:** professional, medium energy, warm, understated, slightly witty.
**Audience:** entrepreneurs, zzp'ers and SMB owners (retail, hospitality) who don't enjoy admin but need it done right.

Characteristics:
- Speaks to the reader directly and informally ("je", "jouw administratie").
- Admits the unglamorous truth first, then reassures — "De administratie. Waarschijnlijk niet je favoriete deel van het ondernemerschap."
- Puts the client at the centre, not the accountant: "administratie waarin jij als ondernemer centraal staat, en niet wij als boekhouder."
- Humanises the team with playful job titles: Factuurvirtuoos, Puntjes op de I-pionier, Chief Financial Onderzoeker, Inboeksysteem Ingenieur, Dossierbouwer, Calamiteiten Commissaris.
- Every team member gets a personal detail and a "kan je 's nachts wakker maken voor" line — warmth is the differentiator.
- Short sentences. Plain Dutch. No jargon, no finance-speak, no hype.

**Avoid:** corporate accountancy clichés, "ontzorgen"-style filler, exclamation-heavy sales tone, anything that makes the social mission sound like charity marketing.

## 7. Messaging

**Core line:** "Goed met cijfers, goed voor mensen."

**Value proposition:** careful, human bookkeeping that adapts to how you run your business — and the work itself creates meaningful jobs for people on the margins of society.

**Pillars**

1. **Zorgvuldigheid** — clients must always be able to trust that the information is correct.
2. **Overzicht** — "Wij creëren het overzicht, zodat jij snel en gemakkelijk de juiste beslissingen kunt maken."
3. **Sociale missie** — "Een systeem werkt voor mensen, niet andersom." With Stichting Nieuw Nabuurschap and Alfa Accountants, Namber offers guidance and meaningful work to people balancing on the edge of society. Choosing Namber = social return on your own bookkeeping spend.

**Proof points:** named team with real portraits, Exact as bookkeeping software, quarterly BTW-aangifte and jaarafsluiting handled in-house, partnership with Alfa Accountants and Stichting Nieuw Nabuurschap.

**CTAs in use:** "Dit is Namber" · "Bekijk onze diensten" · "Bekijk onze missie" · "Neem contact op"
